## Key Clinical & Privacy Boundaries

### 1. Research Proof-of-Concept Framing

- Predictions generated by GlycoTwin are probabilistic risk estimates based on
  statistical patterns in historical CGM and wearable data.
- The system must NOT be described as a clinically validated diagnostic tool or
  therapeutic recommendation engine.
- AUROC 0.757 on a 7-participant test set — not suitable as a clinical decision threshold.

### 2. Scenario Simulator Safeguards

- The Scenario Simulator provides feature-space perturbations for educational
  demonstration only. It is not a physiological or pharmacokinetic simulation.
- Scenarios do NOT constitute medical prescriptions or guaranteed outcome predictions.
- Risk can increase OR decrease under any scenario — this is by design, not a defect.

### 3. Data Privacy & Governance

- Uses PhysioNet CGMacros v1.0.0 — anonymized, date-shifted, no PII.
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — non-commercial research use only.
- No real-world patient data is stored or processed.

### 4. Model Limitations & Scope

- Trained on 45 participants (7 in held-out test set).
- Requires prospective clinical trial validation before any real-world deployment.
- See [SAFETY_AND_LIMITATIONS.md](./SAFETY_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) for the full
  limitations list including threshold selection, calibration, imputation, and
  regulatory requirements.
